# Code of conduct

DroidDock welcomes people with different experience levels, backgrounds, and ways of working. Participate with respect and focus criticism on ideas and behavior, not a person's identity.

## Expected behavior

Be constructive, assume questions can be asked in good faith, respect boundaries, and acknowledge mistakes. Give actionable feedback. Follow the project's privacy rules and keep other people's personal information out of issues, pull requests, screenshots, and examples.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory insults, sexualized content directed at participants, doxxing, and repeated unwanted contact are not acceptable. Neither are spam, impersonation, or knowingly deceptive contribution claims.

## Moderation and reporting

This policy applies in the repository's issues, pull requests, and other project-managed community spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, restrict participation, or block accounts according to the behavior and its severity. Continued violations can lead to removal from the community.

Use GitHub's **Report content** or **Report abuse** controls to report abusive content privately through the platform. Do not repeat sensitive material in a public issue. For ordinary moderation requests that contain no sensitive details, flag the relevant discussion for maintainer attention. Report software vulnerabilities through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not as conduct complaints.

Maintainers are expected to apply these standards consistently and respect the privacy of people involved. GitHub also enforces its own platform policies; this project cannot promise a particular platform outcome or response time.
